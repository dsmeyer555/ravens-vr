Before you go in

The refresh rate only changes if the headset offers it. The report says what you actually got.

In the headset

  1. Left thumbstick skates, slowly. Click it to switch movement off.
  2. Hold both controllers like a stick. The stick runs through both hands.
  3. Hit the puck in front of you. The controllers buzz when the blade touches it.
  4. A steps the scene Light, Medium, Heavy. Give each one about 30 seconds.
  5. B resets the puck. Right thumbstick up and down changes the stick length. Click it to change how low you stand.
  6. The board in front of you shows the live frame rate. If you feel unwell, take the headset off.
