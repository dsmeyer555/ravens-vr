Before you go in

These are saved on this device. Top speed can also be changed inside VR. The refresh rate only changes if the headset offers it, and the report says what you got.

In the headset

  1. Left thumbstick skates. Push the way you want to go, and let go to glide. Push a little for a slow stride and fully for top speed.
  2. Left trigger brakes. Squeeze for a hockey stop. Pulling the thumbstick back against your travel slows you more gently.
  3. Right thumbstick left and right turns you (snap or smooth, as set above). Up and down changes the stick length.
  4. A (right controller) steps the top speed Easy, Normal, Fast. B puts the puck back in front of you.
  5. Y (left controller) sets your height. Sit or stand up straight first, then press it. X leaves VR.
  6. The stick and puck are still the Phase 0 stand-ins. Skate into the boards and the nets: they stop you.
  7. The scoreboard over centre ice shows your speed and the frame rate. If you feel unwell, take the headset off.
  8. When you leave VR, the Last VR session card appears at the bottom of this page. It asks how it felt and is saved if you reload.
