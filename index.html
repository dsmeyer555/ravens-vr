Before you go in

These are saved on this device. Top speed can also be changed inside VR. The refresh rate only changes if the headset offers it, and the report says what you got.

In the headset

  1. Both hands hold the stick. Your top hand (left, for a right shot) is on the butt end and your lower hand slides anywhere along the shaft. Hold the controllers like a real stick. The blade is at the end of the line from your top hand through your lower hand, and turning your top wrist rolls the blade open or closed.
  2. Get into your ready stance with the blade just above the ice and keep it there for a second: the stick calibrates itself so the blade sits flat. Press Y any time to do it again (it also resets your height).
  3. The blade cannot go through the ice: push down and the shaft flattens. The puck is real: it slides, bounces off the boards and glass, rings off the posts and stops in the net.
  4. Left thumbstick skates. Push a little for a slow stride, fully for top speed. In an arc you crossover automatically, and pushing the stick well to the side at speed makes a punch turn (quick, and it costs speed). Left trigger brakes.
  5. Right thumbstick left and right turns you. Up and down sets the stick length. Click it to change curve.
  6. A (right) steps the top speed. B puts the puck back in front of you. X (left) leaves VR.
  7. The scoreboard over centre ice shows your speed, your last hit and best, goals, and the frame rate. If you feel unwell, take the headset off.
  8. When you leave VR, the Last VR session card appears at the bottom of this page. It asks how the stick and puck felt and is saved if you reload.
